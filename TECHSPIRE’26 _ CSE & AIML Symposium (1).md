## About the symposium

TECHSPIRE’26 is a student-focused symposium by the Department of CSE & AIML, held in association with ACM and Techsta Club.

It brings together technical knowledge, creativity, problem-solving and innovation in one day of events. Take part in a technical challenge, a creative one, or both.

Bring your ideas. Showcase your skills. Challenge yourself. Make your mark!
